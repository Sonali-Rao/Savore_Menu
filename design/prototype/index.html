Our kitchen

The food you’d cook every day, if you had the time.

SAVORÉ is a neighbourhood kitchen for weekday lunches, slow Sunday breakfasts and easy dinners. We keep the menu short so everything on it can be made properly — from scratch, each morning.

  • Calories, protein and allergens listed on every dish.
